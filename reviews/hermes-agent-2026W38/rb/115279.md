The plumbing is thorough and matches the repo's conventions: `toolsets.py` gains the entry, `registry.register` moves `write_file`/`patch` to the new toolset, `CONFIGURABLE_TOOLSETS` gains the row, the `debugging` composite includes both tiers, `delegate_tool_toolsets.DEFAULT_TOOLSETS` and the eval harnesses are updated, and the docs explain that this reduces the tool surface rather than sandboxing (a profile with `terminal`/`code_execution` can still write).
