## Overview

This PR introduces a `defer_until_idle` busy policy so session-mutating commands (`compress`, `undo`, `retry`, `save`, `branch`) execute after the active turn commits instead of being rejected, plus a per-session FIFO deferred-command queue on the platform adapter. The dispatch plumbing and FIFO ordering look correct, but the deferred queue has no lifecycle management: entries are never cleared on session reset/stop/shutdown and the queue is unbounded.
