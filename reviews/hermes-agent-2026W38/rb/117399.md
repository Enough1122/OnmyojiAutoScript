1. `agent/memory_manager.py:281-288` (call site `:300`) — a dropped bullet leaves its continuation lines behind, and they are re-parented under the earlier bullet. The loop skips only the offending bullet line (`continue` at :286) while `kept.append(line)` at :288 keeps every other line verbatim, so an indented sub-line that belonged to the removed bullet stays and now reads as a second child of the surviving one. Verified by execution of the post-image function: input `"- prefers draft PRs\n  (logged 12 Jan, source: supermemory)\n- prefers draft PRs\n  (logged 3 Feb, source: builtin)\n"` returns `"- prefers draft PRs\n  (logged 12 Jan, source: supermemory)\n  (logged 3 Feb, source: builtin)\n"` — two distinct memories (and their provenance/timestamps) are merged into one entry, which is worse than the duplicate the PR set out to remove, and the merged text is then stamped into `api_content` and replayed on every later turn. The docstring at :275-277 promises that "headings, prose, blank lines and separators are left exactly as the provider wrote them, so section structure ... survives untouched", but continuation lines are neither considered nor preserved-as-authored. Suggested fix: when a duplicate bullet is dropped, also drop its following continuation block (subsequent lines that are indented deeper than the bullet, up to the next blank line or the next line at the bullet's own indent), or restrict the rule to single-line bullets and leave any bullet that has children untouched.

2. `agent/memory_manager.py:284` — the marker test `stripped[:1] in ("-", "*")` treats markdown emphasis and bold headings as list items. A line such as `**Preferences**` or `*Important:*` starts with `*` and contains alphanumerics, so it enters the dedupe set and a later identical line is silently deleted, contradicting the docstring's claim (:275-277) that headings survive as written. Verified by execution: `"**Preferences**\n- a\n\n**Preferences**\n- b\n"` returns `"**Preferences**\n- a\n\n- b\n"` — the second heading is gone and the section boundary it marked is no longer visible to the model. Suggested fix: require an actual bullet marker followed by whitespace (`re.match(r"[-*+]\s+\S", stripped)`), and exclude lines that begin with `**` (or a `*` that is immediately followed by another `*`/non-space).
